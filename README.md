# WATER-LEVEL-INDICATOR
### Introduction :
A water level indicator is an electronic device used to indicate the level of water in a tank or container. It helps monitor the water level and prevents overflow or emptying of the tank. It uses sensors or metal probes to detect the presence of water at different levels and indicates the level through LEDs or a buzzer.
### Objective :
The main objective of a water level indicator is to monitor the water level in a tank and provide an indication when the water reaches a particular level. It helps prevent water wastage, avoids tank overflow, and supports efficient water management.
### Components Required:
Transistors
Resistors
LEDs
Connecting wires
Breadboard
Power supply or battery
Water level sensing probes
Buzzer (optional)
Water tank or container
### Working :
The water level indicator works on the principle of electrical conductivity of water. Metal probes are placed at different heights inside the tank. When water touches a probe, it completes an electrical path and activates the corresponding transistor circuit. The transistor switches on the LED associated with that water level.
As the water level rises, the LEDs glow one after another, indicating the water level in the tank. When the water reaches the maximum level, the final LED glows, and an optional buzzer sounds to alert the user that the tank is full.
